Sales Order Expiry Date

Track Personal Expiry Dates on Sales Orders

This module adds a personal expiry date field to Sales Orders, allowing you to track expiry dates for personal purposes.

Features

Easy Date Tracking

Add expiry dates directly on sales orders for easy tracking and reference.

Audit Trail

All changes to the expiry date are tracked in the chatter for full audit trail.

Convenient Placement

The expiry date field is conveniently placed just below the Payment Terms field in the sales order form, making it easy to find and update.

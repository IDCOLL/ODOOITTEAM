Sales Order Notes

Add notes field to Sales Orders

Overview

This module adds a convenient notes field to the sales order form, allowing your sales team to capture additional information, special instructions, or important details related to each order.

Features

  • Text field for notes on sales orders
  • Located in the main section of the sales order form for easy access
  • Changes are tracked in the chatter
  • Accessible to all sales team members

Usage

Simply open any sales order and you will find the Order Notes field in the main section. Type any additional information, special instructions, or notes that are relevant to the order.
